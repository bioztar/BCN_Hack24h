# DG ROAST — BCN Hack 24h

Pitch your startup out loud. Two fictional EU officials refuse it — in voice, in
procedure, citing real articles of the EU AI Act, GDPR, NIS2, DORA and friends — and
issue you a stamped Notice of Non-Compliance with a fine calculated from your revenue.

Satire of regulation, not of people. All officials are fictional; every citation is real.

- `docs/PERSONA.md` — the officials, the hard rules, the gag bank (the product)
- `docs/PLAN.md` — build plan, owners, dependencies, acceptance

Grounding corpus: [`bioztar/EU-AI-Act`](https://github.com/bioztar/EU-AI-Act) and
`bioztar/warden`.

Status: pre-event planning. No code yet.
