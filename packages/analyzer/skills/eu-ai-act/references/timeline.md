# Timeline — Compliance Dates & Phase-In

All dates from Art. 113 (application), Art. 111 (legacy systems), Art. 112 (reviews), and in-article deadlines. Entry into force: 1 August 2024 (20th day after OJ publication of 12 July 2024).

**Status check (2026-08-29):** every milestone through **2 Aug 2026** below — prohibitions, AI literacy, GPAI obligations, governance, penalties regime, the Annex III high-risk regime, and Art. 50 transparency — is **already in force**, not upcoming. Only the Annex I product-safety path (2 Aug 2027) and the two 2030/2031 legacy dates remain future as of this check. Read every "applies from" below as a historical trigger date, not a countdown.

## Grace periods for legacy systems (Art. 111)

- **High-risk systems placed on market/put into service before 2 Aug 2026**: Regulation applies only if they undergo **significant changes in their designs** as from that date. Otherwise grandfathered — except the public-authority carve-out above.
- **GPAI models placed before 2 Aug 2025**: providers must take necessary steps to comply by **2 Aug 2027**.
- Prohibited practices (Art. 5) apply regardless of placement date from 2 Feb 2025 — no grandfathering.

## Innovation-support infrastructure
- National AI regulatory sandboxes: at least one per Member State, operational by 2 Aug 2026; may be joint; Commission technical support (Arts. 57–59). Sandbox benefits include priority guidance and possible derogations from real-world-testing conditions (Arts. 58–59, 76(2)).
- Testing in real-world conditions regime with informed consent and MSA supervision (Arts. 60–61, 76).
- SME support: dedicated priorities in QMS/tech-doc simplification, sandbox access priority, dedicated MSA guidance (Arts. 62–63); SME fines capped lower-of (Art. 99(6)).

## Review calendar (Commission, Art. 112)

- Annually from entry into force: assessment of need to amend Annex III and the Art. 5 prohibition list (112(1)).
- 2 Aug 2028 (+ every 4 years): evaluation incl. Annex III headings, transparency list, supervision/governance effectiveness (112(2)); AI Office functioning review (112(5)); standardisation progress on energy-efficient GPAI development (112(6)).
- 2 Aug 2028 (+ every 3 years): impact/effectiveness of voluntary codes of conduct (112(7)).
- 2 Aug 2029 (+ every 4 years): general evaluation report incl. possible Union agency proposal (112(3)).
- 2 Aug 2031: assessment of enforcement, reporting to Parliament/Council/ECOSOC (112(13)).

Delegated acts sunset/renewal cycle runs in five-year periods from 1 Aug 2024 (Art. 97(2)).

## Practical planning note

A product entering development in 2026 targeting an Annex III use case must be conformity-assessment-ready by 2 Aug 2026 — no transition. The 2025 batch (governance + GPAI) arrived first so that infrastructure (AI Office, notified bodies, database) exists when the main obligations bite. Teams integrating GPAI models downstream inherit compliance-relevant information via Annex XII from 2 Aug 2025 onward.
